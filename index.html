Ritajit Majumdar

Research Scientist — IBM Quantum, India Research Lab

Research Interest: Quantum Error Mitigation and Correction, Near-Term Quantum Algorithms, Combinatorial Optimization

My research focuses on making noisy quantum computers useful — developing error mitigation, quantum-classical algorithms, and hardware-aware techniques that bridge the gap between theoretical quantum advantage and practical quantum computation.
