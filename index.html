Ritajit Majumdar

Research Scientist — IBM Quantum, India Research Lab

Research Interest: Quantum Error Mitigation and Correction, Near-Term Quantum Algorithms, Combinatorial Optimization

majumdar.ritajit [at] gmail.com

My work spans co-designing algorithms, error mitigation, and resource-aware execution to make noisy quantum processors reliable, efficient, and useful — through error mitigation and ML-based decoding, hardware-aware algorithm and ansatz design, noise characterization, qubit reuse, and quantum-classical workflows. This is converging toward a quantum-centric supercomputing paradigm: treating QPUs as noise-characterized, schedulable accelerators alongside classical CPU/GPU infrastructure, jointly optimizing quantum resources, classical computation, and error budgets for problems like combinatorial optimization and many-body simulation — moving from isolated demonstrations toward scalable, experimentally grounded quantum utility and advantage.

Quantum Advantage Tracker Our work on non-Abelian hadron dynamics is the first — and to date only — entry from India on the Quantum Advantage Tracker.
